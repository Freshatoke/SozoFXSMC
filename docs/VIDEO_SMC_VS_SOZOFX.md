## Category classification (Phase 4 of Task 12.1's brief)

- **A. Already implemented**: HTF structure direction, LTF CHoCH confirmation, Order Block "location," FVG entry anchor — all exist as first-class SozoFXSMC features (`src/structure/market_structure.py`, `src/features/order_blocks.py`, `src/features/fvg.py`) and are already combined in S2 in a form conceptually very close to the video's trend-continuation idea.
- **B. Partially implemented / implemented differently**: Multi-stage partial exits + structure-based trailing (the `TakeProfitConfig.partial_exits` field and `ManagementConfig.trailing_method="structure"` already exist in `config/settings.py` and are used natively by `run_backtest` — but no S1-S5 strategy's DEFAULT configuration actually uses staged partials the way the video does; this is an existing capability, not a new one, simply not exercised by the current default strategy configs).
- **C. Genuinely new**: The specific combination of (a) no multi-BOS precondition (fires on current structure state alone), (b) FVG-anchored (not OB-anchored) entry, and (c) staged partial+trail management tested together as one strategy — this exact combination does not exist as S1-S5.
- **D. Contradictory to existing logic**: None identified. The video's core mechanic (trend continuation via multi-timeframe alignment) does not contradict S3/S4's reversal logic — they are different, coexistable hypotheses (reversal vs. continuation), not competing claims about the same market behavior.
- **E. Not sufficiently specified**: Volume profile analysis, buyer/seller "battle" narrative reading, DXY correlation confirmation, "candle range theory," and discretionary location/management-trigger selection. None of these have a deterministic equivalent in this codebase, and per `docs/VIDEO_SMC_STRATEGY_SPECIFICATION.md`, none are precisely enough defined even in the source material to build one without inventing rules the video never stated.

## IOS / ITQS / Decision Engine comparison

The video strategy has **no equivalent of ITQS or IOS at all** — its "confluence" is an informal, narrated combination of volume profile + structure + buyer/seller reading, never reduced to a numeric score, and never compared against a portfolio of competing opportunities (the video is a single-trade walkthrough, not a portfolio-selection system). SozoFXSMC's ITQS (Order Block freshness/quality-based trade-quality scoring, validated in Task 9) and IOS (multi-factor opportunity ranking across a live portfolio, validated in Task 10) are strictly more developed than anything the video describes. **S6, as implemented, deliberately does NOT get its own IOS/ITQS treatment beyond what already exists generically for any strategy signal** — it flows through the same `_signal_to_opportunity`/ITQS/IOS pipeline as S1-S5 would, unmodified, when tested in the Decision Engine context (Phase 4's funnel analysis); no new scoring logic was created for S6 specifically, per this task's explicit "do not modify IOS/ITQS" instruction.

## Where the video strategy is a genuinely different bet than S3/S4

S3 and S4 are both **liquidity-sweep mean-reversion** strategies: they bet that an aggressive move beyond a level (PDH/PDL, a swing liquidity pool) is a stop-hunt that will reverse. The video strategy is a **trend-continuation** strategy: it bets that an established directional move will continue after a shallow pullback. These are not the same bet, and a market regime that suppresses one (e.g., a strongly trending market rarely produces the sharp reversals S3/S4 need) can be exactly the regime where the other thrives. This is the honest, structural reason S6 is worth testing as a *complement*, not a replacement — independent of whatever S6's own backtest numbers turn out to be (see `docs/S6_RESEARCH_RESULTS.md`).
